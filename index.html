Benji's Digital Protfolio

Benji has been an active content creator since an early age, artistic and versatile, from meticulously drawing comic books, to end-to-end producing short films and running a few YouTube channels, which have become popular among his friends.

Comic books

Drawn by hand, with endless patience, Benji's comic books are loved by his friends and anyone who came across them.
They are regularly scanned, printed and binded, and shared among friends. Recently their digital copies are also available online to read and download.
Benjis started working on turning them into narrated YouTube videos too.
